## Purpose

This policy sets out how employees may incur, submit and be reimbursed for
business expenses.[^expense-policy-2026-09-01] It exists so that spending
stays consistent, auditable and fair across the organisation, and so that
employees know in advance what will and will not be reimbursed.

## Scope

The policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees, as well as
contractors engaged for a period of one month or longer. It covers travel,
meals, accommodation, client entertainment, and small office purchases made on
the organisation's behalf.

## Reimbursable expenses

- Domestic and international travel booked through the approved travel
  provider.
- Meals during business travel, up to **PKR 3,000 per day**.
- Accommodation at a standard business rate, pre-approved by a line manager.
- Client meals and entertainment, up to **PKR 10,000 per event**, with
  attendee names noted.
- Software, subscriptions, and small office supplies under **PKR 5,000**,
  without prior approval.

## Non-reimbursable expenses

- Personal entertainment, alcohol outside client meals, and family travel
  costs.
- Fines, penalties, or costs arising from personal negligence (e.g. traffic
  tickets).
- Upgrades to travel or accommodation beyond the approved standard tier.

## Submission process

Expenses must be submitted through the finance portal within **14 calendar
days** of being incurred, with an itemised receipt attached to each claim.
Claims submitted after **30 days** will not be reimbursed except where an
exception is granted in writing by the Finance Department.

## Approval

- Claims under **PKR 15,000** require sign-off from the employee's direct
  line manager.
- Claims of **PKR 15,000 or more** require additional approval from the
  Finance Department before reimbursement is processed.
- Approved claims are paid within **7 working days** in the next payroll
  cycle.

## Exceptions

Any exception to this policy — an expense outside the categories above, or a
claim outside the standard submission window — must be requested in writing to
the Finance Department and approved before the expense is incurred wherever
possible.

## Policy review

This policy is reviewed annually by the Finance Department.[^expense-policy-2026-09-01]
Employees will be notified of material changes at least **14 days** before they
take effect. Questions about this policy should be directed to the Finance
Department.

## Open questions

- The approved travel provider is not named, and the policy does not say what
  happens when travel is booked outside it.
- "Standard business rate" and "approved standard tier" are not defined.
- Software or supplies above PKR 5,000 are not mentioned — whether such a
  purchase is reimbursable at all, and through which approval path, is open.
- Alcohol is excluded "outside client meals" — whether it is reimbursable
  within them is not stated.
- Payment timing reads as two clocks at once: "within 7 working days" and "in
  the next payroll cycle". The record does not say which governs.
- Which named role or team in the Finance Department approves claims of PKR
  15,000 and above, and grants exceptions, is not specified.
- The policy states all amounts in PKR; how a foreign-currency expense is
  converted is not covered.
- Line-manager approval is required below PKR 15,000, but the policy does not
  say who approves when the claimant is themselves a line manager or a
  contractor with no line manager.
